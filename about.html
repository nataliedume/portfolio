ABOUT NATALIE

Data makes me curious.

I've always liked questions with more than one layer.

Why did something happen? What patterns are hiding underneath it? How can we turn those patterns into a decision someone can actually use?

That curiosity is what pulled me toward data science. At Baylor University, I studied Data Science alongside Business Administration, giving me the opportunity to explore both the technical side of data and the decisions that data ultimately supports.

Since then, I've worked across machine learning, deep learning, statistical analysis, cybersecurity, financial modeling, visualization and collaborative data projects.

I especially enjoy taking complicated information and turning it into something clearer — whether that's a model, a visualization, an analysis, a process, or a story.
